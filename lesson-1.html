What Is Trading?

Trading is taking a position in a market with the intention of benefiting from price movement. The first thing a beginner needs to understand is that trading is not guessing where price will go.

1. A market has a price

Markets move because buyers and sellers interact. On a chart, that movement is represented by price candles.

If you buy and price rises after your entry, the position can gain value. If price falls, it can lose value.

2. You need a plan before an entry

A responsible trade should answer four questions:

  • What is my setup?
  • Where am I wrong?
  • How much am I risking?
  • Where will I take profit if the idea works?

3. Trading is a probability game

No setup wins every time. A strategy is evaluated over a meaningful sample of trades, not one winning or losing trade.

Your first objective is to learn how to follow a process consistently.
